Supplies

Equipment & Supplies

What each category covers and how it is used in clinical and laboratory settings.

Laboratory Equipment

Supply of equipment for laboratories, hospitals and healthcare facilities.

  • Analyzers

    Automated hematology and clinical chemistry systems for complete blood counts and metabolic, liver, kidney and lipid panels. Batch processing and built-in quality-control tracking support consistent results at high daily volumes.

    • Hematology
    • Clinical chemistry
    • QC tracking

  • Microscopes

    Compound light microscopes for blood film review, parasitology and urine sediment examination, with oil-immersion objectives for fine cellular detail and even LED illumination.

    • Blood films
    • Parasitology
    • Oil immersion

  • Centrifuges

    Benchtop centrifuges that separate serum and plasma and concentrate urine sediment ahead of analysis. Interchangeable rotors fit common tube sizes, with imbalance detection and lid-lock safety.

    • Serum & plasma
    • Multiple tube sizes
    • Lid-lock safety

  • Laboratory accessories

    Pipettes, tube racks, mixers, timers and sample storage that keep daily bench workflow organised, traceable and repeatable.

    • Pipettes
    • Racks & storage
    • Workflow

Laboratory Reagents

Reagents and testing materials for diagnostic laboratories.

  • Diagnostic reagents

    Analyzer-compatible reagents, calibrators and controls for routine chemistry and hematology testing. Each lot is traceable by lot number and expiry date and kept within the manufacturer’s storage conditions, including cold chain where required.

    • Calibrators
    • Controls
    • Lot traceable

  • Testing kits

    Rapid diagnostic tests and laboratory immunoassay kits for infectious disease screening, pregnancy testing and other point-of-care or laboratory use.

    • Rapid tests
    • Immunoassays
    • Point of care

  • Laboratory chemicals

    Stains, fixatives, buffers and cleaning solutions for microscopy, sample preparation and instrument maintenance.

    • Stains
    • Buffers
    • Fixatives

  • Testing materials

    Glass slides, cover slips, urine test strips and quality-control materials that support accurate manual and automated testing.

    • Slides
    • Test strips
    • QC materials

Hospital Consumables

Essential healthcare consumables for hospitals, clinics and laboratories.

  • Gloves

    Disposable examination gloves in nitrile and latex, including powder-free options, for patient care and safe specimen handling.

    • Nitrile
    • Latex
    • Powder-free

  • Syringes

    Sterile, single-use syringes and needles in common volumes for injections and blood collection.

    • Sterile
    • Single-use
    • Common volumes

  • Sample containers

    Colour-coded blood collection tubes with the appropriate additive for each test, plus urine and stool containers and swabs for reliable specimen collection and transport.

    • Blood tubes
    • Urine & stool
    • Swabs

  • Protective supplies

    Face masks, gowns, caps, shoe covers and sharps containers that support infection prevention for staff and patients.

    • Masks & gowns
    • Sharps safety
    • Infection control
